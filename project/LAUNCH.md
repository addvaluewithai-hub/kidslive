# Launch Gate

Launch requires more than an APK/App Store binary.

Before public release KidsLive needs:
- Stable Android and iOS builds.
- At least one polished authored learning world and a second-world architecture validation.
- Progression/rewards with safe persistence/sync.
- Parent/child boundaries and privacy/data-minimization review.
- Bounded AI/tool permissions and failure handling.
- Offline/asset delivery strategy.
- Crash/error/performance observability.
- Accessibility and localization foundations.
- Content QA, store assets/listings, legal/privacy requirements, staged rollout and rollback plan.
