## Critique
- Architecture: provider/model, speech/text, actor, and educational authority boundaries remain one-way and replaceable. `TutorSession` binds observation and guarded mutation to the same `ExperienceEngine`, preventing a second source of educational truth.
- Product/learning: A5 supplies the machinery for an alive tutor without letting generated output determine correctness or progression. The product value must now be proven in A6 through a real learning loop rather than more orchestration abstractions.
- UX/visual: no direct UI/rendering changes were made in A5. A6 must prove that narration, actor behavior, lesson interaction, feedback, and world transitions feel coherent on desktop/mobile.
- Resilience: explicit cancellation, supersede, dispose, timeout, late response, malformed output, provider failure, speech/actor failure, and audit-sink failure have deterministic bounded behavior with stale work rendered inert.
- Performance: A5 added no representative rendering density. Fresh physical Android frame-pacing belongs in A6 once the vertical slice has real lesson/actor/visual density.
- Safety/privacy: tutor proposals remain bounded by A4 guards and tool permissions; approved tool intents are not executed by the tutor bridge. Diagnostics remain data-minimized and avoid unnecessary learner content.

- Production analytics/telemetry persistence and broader privacy policy remain A14 scope; preserve the current data-minimized diagnostic contract.
- Refresh physical Android frame pacing in A6 once the vertical slice reaches representative rendering/interaction density.

## Decision rationale
A5 is safe to close. Its promised orchestration capabilities are implemented behind provider-neutral contracts, all QA blockers were fixed, current main is green across quality, browser stage-gate, and Android packaging evidence, and no live AI/TTS service is required by CI. The remaining items are explicitly scheduled integration/performance/privacy follow-ups that do not undermine A6. A6 can therefore become the current roadmap phase while continuing to treat A4 as educational authority and A5 as bounded orchestration.
